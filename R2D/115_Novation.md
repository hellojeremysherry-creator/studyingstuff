§ 115 Novation
A contract that is itself accepted in satisfaction of a previously existing duty of a third person to the 
promisee is not within the Statute of Frauds as a contract to answer for the duty of another.
## Comments & Illustrations
### Comment
a.  This Section relates to novations.  It makes no difference whether the new promisor promises the same 
performance as that formerly due from the first obligor or a different performance.  The promise is not one to 
answer for another's duty since that other ceases to be under a duty when the new promise becomes binding, 
and the promisor is not a surety.  The case must be distinguished where performance of the new promise -- not 
the promise itself -- is to be taken in satisfaction of the old duty.
