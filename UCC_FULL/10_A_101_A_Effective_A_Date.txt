§ 10-101. Effective Date.
This Act shall become effective at midnight on December 31st following its enactment.  It applies to transactions 
entered into and events occurring after that date.
Annotations
Commentary
Official Comment
This effective date is suggested so that there may be ample time for all those who will be affected by the provisions 
of the Code to become familiar with them.
