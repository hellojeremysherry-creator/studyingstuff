## Definitions And Principles Of Interpretation
## § 1-206. Presumptions
 Whenever [the Uniform Commercial Code] creates a "presumption" with respect to a fact, or provides that a fact is 
"presumed," the trier of fact must find the existence of the fact unless and until evidence is introduced that supports 
a finding of its nonexistence.
