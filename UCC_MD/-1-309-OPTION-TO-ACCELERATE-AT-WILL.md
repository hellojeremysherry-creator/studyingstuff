## Territorial Applicability And General Rules
## § 1-309. Option To Accelerate At Will
 A term providing that one party or that party's successor in interest may accelerate payment or performance or 
require collateral or additional collateral "at will" or when the party "deems itself insecure," or words of similar 
import, means that the party has power to do so only if that party in good faith believes that the prospect of payment 
or performance is impaired. The burden of establishing lack of good faith is on the party against which the power 
has been exercised.
