## U.C.C. § 2-701
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 2. SALES  >  PART 7. REMEDIES
§ 2-701. Remedies for Breach of Collateral Contracts Not Impaired.
Remedies for breach of any obligation or promise collateral or ancillary to a contract for sale are not impaired by the 
provisions of this Article.
