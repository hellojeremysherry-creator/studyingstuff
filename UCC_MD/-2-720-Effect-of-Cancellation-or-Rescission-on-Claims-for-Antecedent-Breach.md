## U.C.C. § 2-720
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 2. SALES  >  PART 7. REMEDIES
§ 2-720. Effect of "Cancellation" or "Rescission" on Claims for Antecedent 
Breach.
Unless the contrary intention clearly appears, expressions of "cancellation" or "rescission" of the contract or the like 
shall not be construed as a renunciation or discharge of any claim in damages for an antecedent breach.
