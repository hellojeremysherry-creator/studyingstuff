## U.C.C. § 2-724
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 2. SALES  >  PART 7. REMEDIES
§ 2-724. Admissibility of Market Quotations.
Whenever the prevailing price or value of any goods regularly bought and sold in any established commodity 
market is in issue, reports in official publications or trade journals or in newspapers or periodicals of general 
circulation published as the reports of such market shall be admissible in evidence.  The circumstances of the 
preparation of such a report may be shown to affect its weight but not its admissibility.
