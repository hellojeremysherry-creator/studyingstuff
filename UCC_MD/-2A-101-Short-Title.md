## U.C.C. § 2A-101
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 2A. LEASES  >  PART 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS
§ 2A-101. Short Title.
This Article shall be known and may be cited as the Uniform Commercial Code-Leases.
See Appendix VI [following Amendment 24 therein] for material relating to changes in the Official Comment to 
conform to the 1990 amendments to various sections of Article 2A.
