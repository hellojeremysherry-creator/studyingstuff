## U.C.C. § 2A-106
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 2A. LEASES  >  PART 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS
§ 2A-106. Limitation on Power of Parties to Consumer Lease to Choose 
Applicable Law and Judicial Forum.
(1)  If the law chosen by the parties to a consumer lease is that of a jurisdiction other than a jurisdiction in 
which the lessee resides at the time the lease agreement becomes enforceable or within 30 days thereafter 
or in which the goods are to be used, the choice is not enforceable.
(2)  If the judicial forum chosen by the parties to a consumer lease is a forum that would not otherwise have 
jurisdiction over the lessee, the choice is not enforceable.
