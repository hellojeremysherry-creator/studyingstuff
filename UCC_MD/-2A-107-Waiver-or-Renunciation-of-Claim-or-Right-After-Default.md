## U.C.C. § 2A-107
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 2A. LEASES  >  PART 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS
§ 2A-107. Waiver or Renunciation of Claim or Right After Default.
Any claim or right arising out of an alleged default or breach of warranty may be discharged in whole or in part 
without consideration by a written waiver or renunciation signed and delivered by the aggrieved party.
