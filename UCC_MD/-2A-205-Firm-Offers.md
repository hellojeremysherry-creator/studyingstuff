## Construction Of Lease Contract
§ 2A-205. Firm Offers.
An offer by a merchant to lease goods to or from another person in a signed writing that by its terms gives 
assurance it will be held open is not revocable, for lack of consideration, during the time stated or, if no time is 
stated, for a reasonable time, but in no event may the period of irrevocability exceed 3 months.  Any such term of 
assurance on a form supplied by the offeree must be separately signed by the offeror.
