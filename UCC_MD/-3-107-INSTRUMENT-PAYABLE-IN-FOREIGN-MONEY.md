## U.C.C. § 3-107
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 3. NEGOTIABLE INSTRUMENTS  >  PART 1. 
## General Provisions And Definitions
## § 3-107. Instrument Payable In Foreign Money
 Unless the instrument otherwise provides, an instrument that states the amount payable in foreign money may be 
paid in the foreign money or in an equivalent amount in dollars calculated by using the current bank-offered spot 
rate at the place of payment for the purchase of dollars on the day on which the instrument is paid.
