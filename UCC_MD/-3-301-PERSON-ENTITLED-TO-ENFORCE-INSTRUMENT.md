## U.C.C. § 3-301
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 3. NEGOTIABLE INSTRUMENTS  >  PART 3. 
## Enforcement Of Instruments
## § 3-301. Person Entitled To Enforce Instrument
 "Person entitled to enforce" an instrument means (i) the holder of the instrument, (ii) a nonholder in possession of 
the instrument who has the rights of a holder, or (iii) a person not in possession of the instrument who is entitled to 
enforce the instrument pursuant to Section 3-309 or 3-418(d). A person may be a person entitled to enforce the 
instrument even though the person is not the owner of the instrument or is in wrongful possession of the instrument.
