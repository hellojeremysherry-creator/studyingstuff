## Items: Depositary And Collecting Banks
## § 4-203. Effect Of Instructions
 Subject to Article 3 concerning conversion of instruments ( Section 3-420) and restrictive indorsements ( Section 3-
206), only a collecting bank's transferor can give instructions that affect the bank or constitute notice to it, and a 
collecting bank is not liable to prior parties for any action taken pursuant to the instructions or in accordance with 
any agreement with its transferor.
