## U.C.C. § 4-404
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 4. BANK DEPOSIT  >  PART 4. RELATIONSHIP 
## Between Payor Bank And Its Customer
## § 4-404. Bank Not Obliged To Pay Check More Than Six Months
OLD
 A bank is under no obligation to a customer having a checking account to pay a check, other than a certified check, 
which is presented more than six months after its date, but it may charge its customer's account for a payment 
made thereafter in good faith.
