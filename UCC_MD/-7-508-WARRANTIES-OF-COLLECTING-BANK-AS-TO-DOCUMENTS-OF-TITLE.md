## U.C.C. § 7-508
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 7. DOCUMENTS OF TITLE  >  PART 5. 
## Warehouse Receipts And Bills Of Lading: Negotiation And Transfer
## § 7-508. Warranties Of Collecting Bank As To Documents Of
## Title
 A collecting bank or other intermediary known to be entrusted with documents of title on behalf of another or with 
collection of a draft or other claim against delivery of documents warrants by the delivery of the documents only its 
own good faith and authority even if the collecting bank or other intermediary has purchased or made advances 
against the claim or draft to be collected.
