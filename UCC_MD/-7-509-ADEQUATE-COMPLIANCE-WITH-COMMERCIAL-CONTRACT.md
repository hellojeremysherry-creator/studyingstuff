## U.C.C. § 7-509
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 7. DOCUMENTS OF TITLE  >  PART 5. 
## Warehouse Receipts And Bills Of Lading: Negotiation And Transfer
## § 7-509. Adequate Compliance With Commercial Contract
 Whether a document of title is adequate to fulfill the obligations of a contract for sale, a contract for lease, or the 
conditions of a letter of credit is determined by Article 2, 2A, or 5.
