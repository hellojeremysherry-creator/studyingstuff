## U.C.C. § 8-113
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 8. INVESTMENT SECURITIES  >  PART 1. SHORT 
## Title And General Matters
## § 8-113. Statute Of Frauds Inapplicable
 A contract or modification of a contract for the sale or purchase of a security is enforceable whether or not there is 
a writing signed or record authenticated by a party against whom enforcement is sought, even if the contract or 
modification is not capable of performance within one year of its making.
