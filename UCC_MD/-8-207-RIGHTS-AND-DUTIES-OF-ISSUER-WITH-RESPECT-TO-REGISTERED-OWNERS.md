## And Issuer
## § 8-207. Rights And Duties Of Issuer With Respect To Registered
## Owners
(a)  Before due presentment for registration of transfer of a certificated security in registered form or of an 
instruction requesting registration of transfer of an uncertificated security, the issuer or indenture trustee 
may treat the registered owner as the person exclusively entitled to vote, receive notifications, and 
otherwise exercise all the rights and powers of an owner.
(b)  This Article does not affect the liability of the registered owner of a security for a call, assessment, or 
the like.
