## U.C.C. § 9-341
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS  >  PART 3. 
## Perfection And Priority  >  Subpart 4. Rights Of Bank
## § 9-341. Bank'S Rights And Duties With Respect To Deposit
## Account
Except as otherwise provided in Section 9-340(c), and unless the bank otherwise agrees in an 
authenticated record, a bank's rights and duties with respect to a deposit account maintained with the bank 
### are not terminated, suspended, or modified by
(1)  the creation, attachment, or perfection of a security interest in the deposit account;
(2)  the bank's knowledge of the security interest; or
(3)  the bank's receipt of instructions from the secured party.
