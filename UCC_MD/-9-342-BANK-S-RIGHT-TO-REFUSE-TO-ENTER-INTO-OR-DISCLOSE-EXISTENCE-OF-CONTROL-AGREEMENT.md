## U.C.C. § 9-342
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS  >  PART 3. 
## Perfection And Priority  >  Subpart 4. Rights Of Bank
## § 9-342. Bank'S Right To Refuse To Enter Into Or Disclose
## Existence Of Control Agreement
 This article does not require a bank to enter into an agreement of the kind described in Section 9-104(a)(2), even if 
its customer so requests or directs. A bank that has entered into such an agreement is not required to confirm the 
existence of the agreement to another person unless requested to do so by its customer.
