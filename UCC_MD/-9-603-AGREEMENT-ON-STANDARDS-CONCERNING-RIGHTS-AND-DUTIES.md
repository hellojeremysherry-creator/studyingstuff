## U.C.C. § 9-603
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS  >  PART 6. 
## Default  >  Subpart 1. Default And Enforcement Of Security Interest
## § 9-603. Agreement On Standards Concerning Rights And Duties
(a) [Agreed standards.]  The parties may determine by agreement the standards measuring the fulfillment 
of the rights of a debtor or obligor and the duties of a secured party under a rule stated in Section 9-602 if 
the standards are not manifestly unreasonable.
(b) [Agreed standards inapplicable to breach of peace.]  Subsection (a) does not apply to the duty 
under Section 9-609 to refrain from breaching the peace.
