## U.C.C. § 9-605
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS  >  PART 6. 
## Default  >  Subpart 1. Default And Enforcement Of Security Interest
## § 9-605. Unknown Debtor Or Secondary Obligor
A secured party does not owe a duty based on its status as secured party:
(1)  to a person that is a debtor or obligor, unless the secured party knows:
(A)  that the person is a debtor or obligor;
(B)  the identity of the person; and
(C)  how to communicate with the person; or
(2)  to a secured party or lienholder that has filed a financing statement against a person, unless the 
### secured party knows
(A)  that the person is a debtor; and
(B)  the identity of the person.
