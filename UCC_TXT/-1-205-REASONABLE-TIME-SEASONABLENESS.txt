DEFINITIONS AND PRINCIPLES OF INTERPRETATION
§ 1-205. REASONABLE TIME; SEASONABLENESS
(a)  Whether a time for taking an action required by [the Uniform Commercial Code] is reasonable depends 
on the nature, purpose, and circumstances of the action.
(b)  An action is taken seasonably if it is taken at or within the time agreed or, if no time is agreed, at or 
within a reasonable time.
