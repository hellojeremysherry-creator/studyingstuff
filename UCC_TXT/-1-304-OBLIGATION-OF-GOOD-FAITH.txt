U.C.C. § 1-304
Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS  >  PART 3. 
TERRITORIAL APPLICABILITY AND GENERAL RULES
§ 1-304. OBLIGATION OF GOOD FAITH
 Every contract or duty within [the Uniform Commercial Code] imposes an obligation of good faith in its performance 
and enforcement.
