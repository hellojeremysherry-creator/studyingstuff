Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS  >  PART 3. 
TERRITORIAL APPLICABILITY AND GENERAL RULES
§ 1-306. WAIVER OR RENUNCIATION OF CLAIM OR RIGHT AFTER BREACH
 A claim or right arising out of an alleged breach may be discharged in whole or in part without consideration by 
agreement of the aggrieved party in an authenticated record.
