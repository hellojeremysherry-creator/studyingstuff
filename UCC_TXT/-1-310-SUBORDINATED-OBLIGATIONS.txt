TERRITORIAL APPLICABILITY AND GENERAL RULES
§ 1-310. SUBORDINATED OBLIGATIONS
 An obligation may be issued as subordinated to performance of another obligation of the person obligated, or a 
creditor may subordinate its right to performance of an obligation by agreement with either the person obligated or 
another creditor of the person obligated. Subordination does not create a security interest as against either the 
common debtor or a subordinated creditor.
