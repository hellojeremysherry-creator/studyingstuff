Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 10. EFFECTIVE DATE AND REPEALER
§ 10-104. Laws Not Repealed.
[(1) ] The Article on Documents of Title (Article 7) does not repeal or modify any laws prescribing the form or 
contents of documents of title or the services or facilities to be afforded by bailees, or otherwise regulating bailees' 
businesses in respects not specifically dealt with herein; but the fact that such laws are violated does not affect the 
status of a document of title which otherwise complies with the definition of a document of title ( Section 1-201).
As amended in 1962 and 1994.
See Appendix XII for material relating to changes made in text in 1994.
