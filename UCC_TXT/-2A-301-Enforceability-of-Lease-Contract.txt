§ 2A-301. Enforceability of Lease Contract.
Except as otherwise provided in this Article, a lease contract is effective and enforceable according to its terms 
between the parties, against purchasers of the goods and against creditors of the parties.
See Appendix VI [following Amendment 24 therein] for material relating to changes in the Official Comment to 
conform to the 1990 amendments to various sections of Article 2A.
