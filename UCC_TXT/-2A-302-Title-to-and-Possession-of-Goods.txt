§ 2A-302. Title to and Possession of Goods.
Except as otherwise provided in this Article, each provision of this Article applies whether the lessor or a third party 
has title to the goods, and whether the lessor, the lessee, or a third party has possession of the goods, 
notwithstanding any statute or rule of law that possession or the absence of possession is fraudulent.
