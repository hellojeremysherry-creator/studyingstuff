§ 2A-407. Irrevocable Promises: Finance Leases.
(1)  In the case of a finance lease that is not a consumer lease the lessee's promises under the lease 
contract become irrevocable and independent upon the lessee's acceptance of the goods.
(2)  A promise that has become irrevocable and independent under subsection (1):
(a)  is effective and enforceable between the parties, and by or against third parties including assignees 
of the parties; and
(b)  is not subject to cancellation, termination, modification, repudiation, excuse, or substitution without 
the consent of the party to whom the promise runs.
(3)  This section does not affect the validity under any other law of a covenant in any lease contract making 
the lessee's promises irrevocable and independent upon the lessee's acceptance of the goods.
