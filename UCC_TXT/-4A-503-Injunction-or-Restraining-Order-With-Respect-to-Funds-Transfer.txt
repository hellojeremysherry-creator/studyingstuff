MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
§ 4A-503. Injunction or Restraining Order With Respect to Funds Transfer.
For proper cause and in compliance with applicable law, a court may restrain (i) a person from issuing a payment 
order to initiate a funds transfer, (ii) an originator's bank from executing the payment order of the originator, or (iii) 
the beneficiary's bank from releasing funds to the beneficiary or the beneficiary from withdrawing the funds.  A court 
may not otherwise restrain a person from issuing a payment order, paying or receiving payment of a payment order, 
or otherwise acting with respect to a funds transfer.
