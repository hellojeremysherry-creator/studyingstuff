Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 7. DOCUMENTS OF TITLE  >  PART 2. 
WAREHOUSE RECEIPTS: SPECIAL PROVISIONS
§ 7-205. TITLE UNDER WAREHOUSE RECEIPT DEFEATED IN CERTAIN 
CASES
 A buyer in ordinary course of business of fungible goods sold and delivered by a warehouse that is also in the 
business of buying and selling such goods takes the goods free of any claim under a warehouse receipt even if the 
receipt is negotiable and has been duly negotiated.
