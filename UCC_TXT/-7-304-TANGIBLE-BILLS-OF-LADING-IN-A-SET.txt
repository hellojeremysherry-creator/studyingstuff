§ 7-304. TANGIBLE BILLS OF LADING IN A SET
(a)  Except as customary in international transportation, a tangible bill of lading may not be issued in a set 
of parts. The issuer is liable for damages caused by violation of this subsection.
(b)  If a tangible bill of lading is lawfully issued in a set of parts, each of which contains an identification 
code and is expressed to be valid only if the goods have not been delivered against any other part, the 
whole of the parts constitutes one bill.
(c)  If a tangible negotiable bill of lading is lawfully issued in a set of parts and different parts are negotiated 
to different persons, the title of the holder to which the first due negotiation is made prevails as to both the 
document of title and the goods even if any later holder may have received the goods from the carrier in 
good faith and discharged the carrier's obligation by surrendering its part.
(d)  A person that negotiates or transfers a single part of a tangible bill of lading issued in a set is liable to 
holders of that part as if it were the whole set.
(e)  The bailee shall deliver in accordance with Part 4 against the first presented part of a tangible bill of 
lading lawfully issued in a set. Delivery in this manner discharges the bailee's obligation on the whole bill.
