WAREHOUSE RECEIPTS AND BILLS OF LADING: NEGOTIATION AND TRANSFER
§ 7-505. INDORSER NOT GUARANTOR FOR OTHER PARTIES
 The indorsement of a tangible document of title issued by a bailee does not make the indorser liable for any default 
by the bailee or previous indorsers.
