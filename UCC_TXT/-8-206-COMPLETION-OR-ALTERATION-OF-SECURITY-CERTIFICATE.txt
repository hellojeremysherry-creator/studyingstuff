§ 8-206. COMPLETION OR ALTERATION OF SECURITY CERTIFICATE
(a)  If a security certificate contains the signatures necessary to its issue or transfer but is incomplete in any 
other respect:
(1)  any person may complete it by filling in the blanks as authorized; and
(2)  even if the blanks are incorrectly filled in, the security certificate as completed is enforceable by a 
purchaser who took it for value and without notice of the incorrectness.
(b)  A complete security certificate that has been improperly altered, even if fraudulently, remains 
enforceable, but only according to its original terms.
