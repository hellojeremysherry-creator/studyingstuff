Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 8. INVESTMENT SECURITIES  >  PART 4. 
REGISTRATION
§ 8-406. OBLIGATION TO NOTIFY ISSUER OF LOST, DESTROYED, OR 
WRONGFULLY TAKEN SECURITY CERTIFICATE
 If a security certificate has been lost, apparently destroyed, or wrongfully taken, and the owner fails to notify the 
issuer of that fact within a reasonable time after the owner has notice of it and the issuer registers a transfer of the 
security before receiving notification, the owner may not assert against the issuer a claim for registering the transfer 
under Section 8-404 or a claim to a new security certificate under Section 8-405.
