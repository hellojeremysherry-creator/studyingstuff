Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 8. INVESTMENT SECURITIES  >  PART 5. 
SECURITY ENTITLEMENTS
§ 8-502. ASSERTION OF ADVERSE CLAIM AGAINST ENTITLEMENT HOLDER
 An action based on an adverse claim to a financial asset, whether framed in conversion, replevin, constructive 
trust, equitable lien, or other theory, may not be asserted against a person who acquires a security entitlement 
under Section 8-501 for value and without notice of the adverse claim.
