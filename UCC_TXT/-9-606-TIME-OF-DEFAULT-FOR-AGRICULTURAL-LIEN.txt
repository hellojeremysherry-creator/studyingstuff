Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)  >  ARTICLE 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS  >  PART 6. 
DEFAULT  >  SUBPART 1. DEFAULT AND ENFORCEMENT OF SECURITY INTEREST
§ 9-606. TIME OF DEFAULT FOR AGRICULTURAL LIEN
 For purposes of this part, a default occurs in connection with an agricultural lien at the time the secured party 
becomes entitled to enforce the lien in accordance with the statute under which it was created.
