TRANSITION PROVISIONS FOR 2010 AMENDMENTS
§ 9-809. PRIORITY
 This [Act] determines the priority of conflicting claims to collateral. However, if the relative priorities of the claims 
were established before this [Act] takes effect, [Article 9 as it existed before amendment] determines priority.
