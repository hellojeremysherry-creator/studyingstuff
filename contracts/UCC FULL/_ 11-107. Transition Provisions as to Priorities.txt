TRANSITION PROVISIONS        
§ 11-107. Transition Provisions as to Priorities.
Except as otherwise provided in [Article 11], [old U.C.C.] shall apply to any questions of priority if the positions of 
the parties were fixed prior to the effective date of [new U.C.C.].  In other cases questions of priority shall be 
determined by [new U.C.C.].
