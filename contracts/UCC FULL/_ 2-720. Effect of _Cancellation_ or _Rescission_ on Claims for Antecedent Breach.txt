§ 2-720. Effect of "Cancellation" or "Rescission" on Claims for Antecedent 
Breach.
Unless the contrary intention clearly appears, expressions of "cancellation" or "rescission" of the contract or the like 
shall not be construed as a renunciation or discharge of any claim in damages for an antecedent breach.
Annotations
Commentary
Official Comment
Prior Uniform Statutory Provision: None.
Purpose:
This section is designed to safeguard a person holding a right of action from any unintentional loss of rights by the 
ill-advised use of such terms as "cancellation", "rescission", or the like.  Once a party's rights have accrued they are 
not to be lightly impaired by concessions made in business decency and without intention to forego them.  
Therefore, unless the cancellation of a contract expressly declares that it is "without reservation of rights", or the 
like, it cannot be considered to be a renunciation under this section.
