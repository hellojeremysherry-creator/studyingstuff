>            PART 2. ISSUE AND ISSUER        
§ 8-209. ISSUER'S LIEN
 A lien in favor of an issuer upon a certificated security is valid against a purchaser only if the right of the issuer to 
the lien is noted conspicuously on the security certificate.
Annotations
Commentary
OFFICIAL COMMENT
   This section is similar to Sections 8-202 and 8-204 which require that the terms of a certificated security and any 
restriction on transfer imposed by the issuer be noted on the security certificate. This section differs from those two 
sections in that the purchaser's know ledge of the issuer's claim is irrelevant. "Noted" makes clear that the text of 
the lien provisions need not be set forth in full. However, this would not override a provision of an applicable 
corporation code requiring statement in haec verba. This section does not apply to uncertificated securities. It 
applies to the indirect holding system in the same fashion as Sections 8-202 and 8-204, see Comment 2 to Section 
8-202.
