§ 9-402. SECURED PARTY NOT OBLIGATED ON CONTRACT OF DEBTOR 
OR IN TORT
 The existence of a security interest, agricultural lien, or authority given to a debtor to dispose of or use collateral, 
without more, does not subject a secured party to liability in contract or tort for the debtor's acts or omissions.
Annotations
Commentary
OFFICIAL COMMENT
   1. Source. Former Section 9-317.
   2. Nonliability of Secured Party. This section, like former Section 9-317, rejects theories on which a secured 
party might be held liable on a debtor's contracts or in tort merely because a security interest exists or because the 
debtor is entitled to dispose of or use collateral. This section expands former Section 9-317 to cover agricultural 
liens.
