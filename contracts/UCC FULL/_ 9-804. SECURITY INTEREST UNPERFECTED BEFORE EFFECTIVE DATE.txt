§ 9-804. SECURITY INTEREST UNPERFECTED BEFORE EFFECTIVE DATE.
A security interest that is an unperfected security interest immediately before this [Act] takes effect 
becomes a perfected security interest:
(1)  without further action, when this [Act] takes effect if the applicable requirements for perfection 
under [Article 9 as amended by this [Act]] are satisfied before or at that time; or
(2)  when the applicable requirements for perfection are satisfied if the requirements are satisfied after 
that time.
