§ 2-707. "Person in the Position of a Seller".
(1)  A "person in the position of a seller" includes as against a principal an agent who has paid or become 
responsible for the price of goods on behalf of his principal or anyone who otherwise holds a security 
interest or other right in goods similar to that of a seller.
(2)  A person in the position of a seller may as provided in this Article withhold or stop delivery ( Section 2-
705) and resell ( Section 2-706) and recover incidental damages ( Section 2-710).
Annotations
Commentary
Official Comment
Prior Uniform Statutory Provision: Section 52(2), Uniform Sales Act.
Changes: Rewritten.
Purposes of Changes: To make it clear that:
In addition to following in general the prior uniform statutory provision, the case of a financing agency which has 
acquired documents by honoring a letter of credit for the buyer or by discounting a draft for the seller has been 
included in the term "a person in the position of a seller."
