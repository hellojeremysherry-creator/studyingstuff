U.C.C. § 3-114
          Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)          >            ARTICLE 3. NEGOTIABLE INSTRUMENTS          
>            PART 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS        
§ 3-114. CONTRADICTORY TERMS OF INSTRUMENT
 If an instrument contains contradictory terms, typewritten terms prevail over printed terms, handwritten terms 
prevail over both, and words prevail over numbers.
Annotations
Commentary
OFFICIAL COMMENT
   Section 3-114 replaces subsections (b) and (c) of former Section 3-118.
