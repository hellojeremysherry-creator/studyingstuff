          Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)          >            ARTICLE 4. BANK DEPOSIT          >            
PART 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS        
§ 4-111. STATUTE OF LIMITATIONS
 An action to enforce an obligation, duty, or right arising under this Article must be commenced within three years 
after the [cause of action] accrues.
Annotations
Commentary
OFFICIAL COMMENT
   This section conforms to the period of limitations set by Section 3-118(g) for actions for breach of warranty and to 
enforce other obligations, duties or rights arising under Article 3. Bracketing "cause of action" recognizes that some 
states use a different term, such as "claim for relief."
