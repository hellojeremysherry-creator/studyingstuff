          Uniform Commercial Code (U.C.C.)          >            ARTICLE 7. DOCUMENTS OF TITLE          >            
PART 7. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS        
§ 7-702. REPEALS
 [Existing Article 7] and [Section 10-104 of the Uniform Commercial Code] are repealed.
Annotations
Commentary
OFFICIAL COMMENT
   A state should repeal its prior version of Uniform Commercial Code Article 7 on documents of title and Uniform 
Commercial Code section 10-204. The substance of Section 10-104 has been incorporated into Section 7-103(b).
