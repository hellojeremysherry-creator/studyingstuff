TRANSITION PROVISIONS        
§ 11-104. Transition Provision on Change of Requirement of Filing.
A security interest for the perfection of which filing or the taking of possession was required under [old U.C.C.] and 
which attached prior to the effective date of [new U.C.C.] but was not perfected shall be deemed perfected on the 
effective date of [new U.C.C.] if [new U.C.C.] permits perfection without filing or authorizes filing in the office or 
offices where a prior ineffective filing was made.
