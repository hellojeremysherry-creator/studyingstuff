2. FORMATION AND CONSTRUCTION OF LEASE CONTRACT        
§ 2A-204. Formation in General.
(1)  A lease contract may be made in any manner sufficient to show agreement, including conduct by both 
parties which recognizes the existence of a lease contract.
(2)  An agreement sufficient to constitute a lease contract may be found although the moment of its making 
is undetermined.
(3)  Although one or more terms are left open, a lease contract does not fail for indefiniteness if the parties 
have intended to make a lease contract and there is a reasonably certain basis for giving an appropriate 
remedy.
